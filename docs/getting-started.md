# Getting started with Breadcrumb

[Open Breadcrumb](https://bonitadreama.github.io/breadcrumb/) in your usual browser. No account is needed. Your first journal is ready automatically; rename it or add another using **Profiles** or the journal name at the top.

## Leave a breadcrumb

1. Choose **Something feels off**, another quick action, or **Something happened**.
2. Use right now, choose an earlier time, or give an approximate window. You do not need to remember the exact minute.
3. Write as much or as little as you want. Optionally add a photo and caption; **Annotate photo** offers freehand, arrows, and circles.
4. Choose **Save breadcrumb** and wait for confirmation before closing the page. Unsaved form drafts are not automatically saved.

Choose an entry on **Your timeline** to read it, edit it, or open its photos. **Look Back around this** gathers entries before and after that moment without filling in missing information or diagnosing a pattern.

## Keep a copy

Choose **Backups → Download backup**. Keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere private. It includes all local profiles, photos, and edit history and is not encrypted. On Android, look in the Files app's Downloads folder.

To recover a backup or copy it to another device, open Breadcrumb there, choose **Backups → Choose backup file**, review the preview, and confirm. Different versions of the same record will not overwrite each other; a conflict stops restoration. Copies on different devices do not synchronize.

Clearing browser/site data or losing a device can lose your journal. Local profiles are organizational containers, not password-protected accounts. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to read them. Corrections and removed photos remain in history and backups. Permanent erasure within the app is not implemented.

## Android Chrome check

Open the HTTPS app link directly in Chrome. First use fictional notes and a nonpersonal photo:

1. Save a note with a photo, caption, and finger-drawn mark.
2. Close and reopen the page; check the entry, photo orientation, and marks.
3. Open Look Back and zoom the photo.
4. Download a backup. Restore it in a separate browser or browser profile, then check the entry and photo. Keep the original journal intact while testing.
5. Once the app has loaded online, try Chrome's **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** option if offered. Reopen it offline to check cached app availability. Installation wording and availability depend on the browser/device; a normal browser bookmark also works.

The app supports still JPEG, PNG, and WebP (six per observation, up to 8 MiB / 40 megapixels each). HEIC must be converted first. Originals are preserved and may include camera/location metadata. The current shared capacity is 48 MiB of encoded photos across all local journals and history.

## Help make it better

This is an early community preview. [Report a problem or share an idea](https://github.com/bonitadreama/breadcrumb/issues) using fictional examples. Never post journal backups, medical photos, or identifying health details. Report security vulnerabilities privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** link.

Breadcrumb helps you remember. It does not diagnose, give treatment advice, or provide medication alarms. It's okay to be sick, and you don't have to log perfectly.
